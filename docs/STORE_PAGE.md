## About the game
Shieldbreak is a classic arena shooter where every fight is decided by your shield. It soaks damage and recharges
when you stop getting hit, so fights are about timing: break their shield, then finish the job before it comes back.

- **Shield mastery.** Vent your shield at the right moment for an instant refill, or trigger a Break Pulse the instant
  your shield pops to knock attackers back.
- **A tight sandbox.** Battle rifle and sidearm on spawn; sniper, rockets, the charge-shot Rail Lance, the Aegis
  frontal barrier, active camo and overshield on the map. Power weapons respawn on a timer: control them to win.
- **Movement with momentum.** Sprint, slide and carry the speed into a jump, or charge a Power Jump to reach high
  ground.
- **Five handcrafted maps**, each laid out for its modes: Keel, Bastion, Sanctum, Creekbed and Spire.
- **Four modes**: Team Slayer, Capture the Flag, King of the Hill and Oddball.
- **Bots** with four skill levels, so you can play any mode alone.
- **LAN and direct-IP multiplayer** on listen servers.
- **Medals** for multi-kills, sprees, headshots and more.
- Full controller support with aim assist, key rebinding, and graphics settings that scale down to laptops.
